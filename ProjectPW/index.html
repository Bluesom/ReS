Our Story

Rooted in the Dolomites

Set beside Lake Misurina, Grand Hotel Misurina is part of the area's tradition of alpine hospitality. Its lakeside setting offers a peaceful base for discovering the surrounding peaks, trails, and mountain villages.

Destinations

01 South Tyrol, Italy

Lago di Braies

Wooden boats glide across turquoise water beneath forested slopes and limestone peaks. Walk the lakeshore for a quieter view of the Dolomites.

02 South Tyrol, Italy

Alpe di Siusi

Open alpine meadows stretch beneath the jagged Sassolungo peaks. The high plateau offers a broad, peaceful perspective on the Dolomites.

03 Sexten Dolomites, Italy

Tre Cime di Lavaredo

Three limestone towers rise above celebrated high-mountain trails. Follow the paths for changing views of one of the Dolomites' most recognizable silhouettes.
